# M gate (semantic) contract

Scope: meaning of one sentence. M answers "does this sentence hold together in meaning?"

Must flag as anomalous (M never blocks on this alone; the funnel decides):
- Coordinated contradictions about one subject (The door was open and closed. The cat is alive and dead.).
- Self-contradictory modifier-noun pairs (The square circle rolled down the hill.).
- Selectional violations: an inanimate subject with a verb that needs an animate agent (The rock eats a sandwich. The idea sleeps soundly.).

Must pass (literal, coherent):
- Ordinary literal sentences (The table is made of oak. She bought fresh bread this morning.).
- Literal descriptions of weather and place (The fog drifts through the valley.).

Known limit, reported separately and not required for now: metaphor (Hope floats in the human heart.). Metaphor may flag as anomalous. That is allowed.

Does not judge: syntax (S), harm (Q), or context across sentences (P).
Output: coherent, or anomalous with the rule that fired.
